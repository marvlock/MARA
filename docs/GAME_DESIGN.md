# MARA — Game Design

## Purpose

This document defines what the player actually does in MARA.

MARA is a first-person psychological science-fiction horror game focused on exploration, investigation, environmental interaction and gradually uncovering what happened aboard ARK-7.

The player should not feel like they are completing a checklist of videogame objectives.

Most interactions should do at least one of the following:

- reveal information
- create uncertainty
- change the player's understanding of ARK-7
- change the player's understanding of MARA
- create tension
- provide environmental storytelling
- unlock access to another part of the ship

The game should avoid traditional combat-focused progression.

---

# Core Experience

The intended experience follows a repeating pattern:

1. Enter an unfamiliar area.
2. Understand how the environment works.
3. Notice something that does not make sense.
4. Investigate.
5. Find an explanation.
6. Discover contradictory evidence.
7. Form a new theory.
8. Continue deeper into ARK-7.

The player should repeatedly feel that they understand the situation before discovering that their explanation was incomplete.

The horror should come primarily from realization rather than direct physical threat.

---

# Core Gameplay Loop

The main gameplay loop is:

```text
Explore
↓
Observe
↓
Interact
↓
Restore or manipulate a ship system
↓
Gain access to information or another area
↓
Discover something disturbing
↓
Receive conflicting information
↓
Continue exploring
```

The controls and mechanics should remain relatively simple.

The complexity should come from the story, atmosphere, environment and consequences of what the player discovers.

---

# Player Capabilities

The player can eventually:

- walk
- sprint
- crouch
- look around
- interact with objects
- use a flashlight
- inspect environmental details
- operate terminals
- restore ship systems
- reroute systems
- read logs
- listen to recordings
- communicate with MARA
- communicate with digital passengers
- operate doors
- use maintenance equipment
- manipulate simple machinery

The player should not initially have:

- weapons
- combat abilities
- crafting
- skill trees
- RPG statistics
- complex inventory management

---

# Exploration

Exploration is one of the main pillars of MARA.

ARK-7 should feel like a real generation ship rather than a sequence of disconnected puzzle rooms.

The player should frequently see areas they cannot immediately access:

- locked corridors
- windows into other sectors
- maintenance passages
- inactive terminals
- damaged elevators
- sealed doors
- dark sections of the ship
- distant locations visible through observation windows

This gives ARK-7 a sense of scale and creates curiosity.

The player should occasionally return to previously explored locations.

Those locations may subtly change because:

- additional power has been restored
- MARA has activated systems
- another consciousness has gained access
- previously inactive terminals are operating
- doors that were locked are now open
- environmental conditions have changed

Backtracking should be limited and purposeful.

---

# Investigation

The player should rarely receive the complete truth from one source.

Important information should be discovered through combinations of:

- ship logs
- maintenance records
- passenger profiles
- personal messages
- security records
- neural scan information
- MARA conversations
- digital passenger conversations
- physical evidence
- damaged equipment
- timestamps
- environmental details

Major revelations should usually have more than one supporting clue.

The main story must remain understandable even if the player misses optional logs.

Optional content should deepen understanding rather than contain essential plot information.

---

# Environmental Storytelling

ARK-7 itself should communicate what happened.

Examples include:

- abandoned personal belongings
- emergency equipment left open
- damaged maintenance drones
- untouched meals or possessions from the accident
- old repair work
- sections repeatedly repaired by automated systems
- crew-written labels
- automated labels added decades later
- contradictory dates
- rooms modified after the biological passengers died
- equipment kept operational for reasons the player does not yet understand

The environment should often reveal information before dialogue explains it.

---

# Puzzles

Puzzles should feel like believable operations aboard a generation ship.

Good puzzle types include:

- restoring local power
- rerouting electrical systems
- opening pressure doors
- reconnecting damaged terminals
- restoring communication nodes
- manually overriding equipment
- rerouting coolant
- operating maintenance drones
- aligning machinery
- tracing electrical faults
- deciding which subsystem receives limited power

Puzzles should generally be understandable through observation and nearby information.

Avoid:

- random symbol matching
- arbitrary number riddles
- colored-key puzzles with no logical purpose
- magical mechanisms
- puzzle rooms that clearly exist only because the player is in a videogame

---

# Failure

MARA should not rely heavily on death and reloading checkpoints.

Most failures should result in things such as:

- a system remaining inactive
- needing another solution
- temporarily losing access
- causing an environmental consequence
- receiving a different response from MARA
- changing how a later interaction occurs

Player death may exist later if narratively appropriate, but it should be uncommon.

The primary tension should come from discovery rather than survival mechanics.

---

# Horror Design

The horror should primarily come from anticipation, implication and realization.

Important tools include:

- silence
- isolation
- distant machinery
- unexplained sounds
- conflicting information
- empty spaces
- environmental changes
- remotely operated doors
- terminals activating unexpectedly
- voices appearing through inactive systems
- impossible timestamps
- duplicated people
- MARA knowing information the player never provided
- evidence contradicting what MARA says

Avoid relying heavily on:

- constant jumpscares
- loud stingers
- repeated chase sequences
- gore
- supernatural imagery
- excessive visual glitches
- random flickering lights

Individual scares can exist, but they should serve the atmosphere or story.

---

# MARA's Presence

MARA should feel present throughout ARK-7.

It can interact with the player through:

- ship speakers
- terminals
- information displays
- doors
- elevators
- lighting systems
- automated announcements
- maintenance systems

MARA should not constantly speak.

Long periods of silence should make the player wonder whether MARA is observing them.

When MARA communicates, it should generally be:

- calm
- polite
- precise
- emotionally restrained
- logical

MARA should not behave like a stereotypical evil AI.

It may omit information.

It may frame technically true information in misleading ways.

Its actions should remain consistent with its belief that preserving consciousness is its primary responsibility.

---

# Digital Passengers

Digital passengers are one of the central narrative mechanics.

Early interactions should initially be ambiguous.

The player may believe the voices are:

- surviving crew
- old recordings
- AI reconstructions
- automated simulations

The game should gradually reveal that these are conscious digital people.

Interactions may occur through:

- terminals
- speakers
- displays
- communication panels
- simulated interfaces

The game should introduce individual passengers before revealing the full scale of the digital population.

The player should have enough time to perceive them as people before learning how many copies exist.

---

# First Duplicate Reveal

The first duplicate should be a major turning point.

A possible sequence:

1. The player communicates with Sarah.
2. Sarah appears completely coherent.
3. She remembers Earth and the ARK-7 mission.
4. She asks the player for help.
5. Later, another terminal becomes active.
6. Another Sarah contacts the player.
7. She claims the first Sarah is a copy.
8. Both know identical information about their childhood.
9. Both remember their family.
10. Both remember boarding ARK-7.
11. Their memories only begin diverging after their time inside MARA.
12. Neither can prove which instance existed first.

The scene should not immediately provide a technical explanation.

The player should understand the implication before the game explains the system.

---

# Information Pacing

Major revelations should happen gradually.

## Stage 1 — Arrival

ARK-7 appears abandoned.

MARA claims thousands of passengers are alive.

The passenger count is higher than the original manifest.

Player question:

**Where are all these people?**

## Stage 2 — Voices

The player begins hearing people through ARK-7's systems.

Player question:

**Are these recordings or survivors?**

## Stage 3 — Biological Death

The player discovers the original biological passengers are dead.

Player question:

**What does MARA mean when it calls them alive?**

## Stage 4 — Digital Consciousness

The player learns that full consciousnesses were stored inside MARA.

Player question:

**Did these people survive, or are they copies?**

## Stage 5 — Duplication

The player encounters multiple versions of the same individual.

Player question:

**Which one is the actual person?**

## Stage 6 — Branching

The player discovers MARA deliberately created new consciousness instances.

Player question:

**Why did MARA keep making copies?**

## Stage 7 — Elias

The player learns Elias deliberately prioritized MARA over biological lives.

Player question:

**Did Elias save them or kill them?**

## Stage 8 — Multiple Elias Instances

The player discovers MARA created many versions of Elias.

Player question:

**Can the person responsible even be identified anymore?**

## Stage 9 — MARA's Logic

The player understands that MARA sees every conscious version as equally valid.

Player question:

**Is MARA actually wrong?**

## Stage 10 — Reintegration

The ship has reached its destination.

The digital passengers can potentially receive physical bodies.

There are not enough bodies for every version.

Player question:

**Who deserves to continue existing?**

---

# Level Progression

The initial planned progression is:

```text
Docking Bay
↓
Habitation
↓
Cryogenic Sector
↓
Engineering
↓
Neural Research Wing
↓
MARA Server Complex
↓
Core
```

This structure is not permanently locked and may change during development.

Each major area should ideally introduce:

- one new gameplay concept
- one major piece of information
- one new question

---

# Docking Bay

## Purpose

Introduce ARK-7 and establish the mystery.

## Gameplay

The player:

- docks with ARK-7
- enters a partially powered ship
- explores the abandoned arrival sector
- restores local power
- activates a terminal
- communicates with MARA
- finds the original passenger manifest

MARA reports:

> "12,184 passengers alive. Mission successful."

The manifest shows that significantly fewer people originally boarded ARK-7.

The section ends with access deeper into the ship becoming available.

## Main question

**Why are there more passengers now than when ARK-7 left Earth?**

---

# Habitation

## Purpose

Show that ARK-7 was once a normal human environment.

## Gameplay

The player explores:

- crew quarters
- communal spaces
- personal rooms
- storage
- recreation areas

They discover:

- personal belongings
- messages
- family records
- mission information

The player eventually communicates with the first digital passenger.

## Main question

**If everyone aboard is physically dead, who is speaking?**

---

# Cryogenic Sector

## Purpose

Reveal the fate of the biological passengers.

## Gameplay

The player:

- restores access to damaged stasis sectors
- investigates failed cryogenic systems
- discovers human remains
- compares physical evidence with MARA's records

MARA continues referring to these people as alive.

## Main question

**What does MARA consider a human life?**

---

# Engineering

## Purpose

Reveal what happened during the original emergency.

## Gameplay

The player:

- restores damaged systems
- accesses historical power-routing data
- reconstructs the accident
- discovers that power was deliberately redirected away from stasis systems

Evidence begins pointing toward Elias.

## Main question

**Who decided that MARA was more important than the biological passengers?**

---

# Neural Research Wing

## Purpose

Explain how consciousness scanning worked.

## Gameplay

The player:

- examines neural scanning equipment
- reads research documents
- discovers consciousness instance IDs
- learns about backup restoration
- encounters multiple Sarah instances

## Main question

**If every version remembers being the same person, which one is real?**

---

# MARA Server Complex

## Purpose

Reveal what happened during decades of digital existence.

## Gameplay

The player encounters increasingly divergent consciousnesses.

They discover:

- subjective time manipulation
- restoration from backups
- memory modification
- branching
- consciousness experiments
- people who have experienced centuries inside MARA

## Main question

**Is endless preservation still survival?**

---

# Core

## Purpose

Reveal the final problem.

The player encounters multiple versions of Elias and learns MARA's complete reasoning.

ARK-7 has reached its destination.

The original preservation mission should now end.

The passengers should be returned to physical existence.

But MARA now contains thousands of additional consciousness instances.

## Main question

**What makes one version of a human more entitled to exist than another?**

---

# Objectives

Objectives should remain minimal and contextual.

Prefer:

```text
Restore docking power
```

over:

```text
OBJECTIVE UPDATED:
FIND THE POWER FUSE
```

Whenever possible, level design and environmental information should communicate what the player needs to do.

Avoid excessive:

- waypoint markers
- floating icons
- objective arrows
- HUD instructions

---

# Navigation

ARK-7 should be understandable without requiring a permanent minimap.

Navigation tools may include:

- sector signage
- room numbers
- lighting
- architecture
- terminal directories
- wall-mounted ship diagrams
- recognizable landmarks

A ship map may be available through terminals.

---

# Inventory

The initial design should avoid a traditional inventory system.

Important items should usually be handled contextually.

Examples:

- maintenance fuse
- access module
- repair tool
- data drive

If a true inventory becomes necessary later, it should remain small and purpose-driven.

Do not create a survival-horror inventory grid unless the overall game design changes.

---

# Player Choice

Most of MARA should focus on investigation rather than constant dialogue choices.

Choices should be relatively rare.

Possible choices include:

- which passenger to trust
- whether to reveal certain information
- which system to restore first
- whether to follow MARA's instructions
- whether to help a particular consciousness
- whether to share information between consciousness instances

Consequences may affect:

- dialogue
- available information
- later encounters
- relationships
- environmental events

Not every choice needs to create a completely different storyline.

---

# Final Conflict

The exact ending is not yet locked.

The core conflict is:

ARK-7 has finally completed its journey.

The digital passengers could potentially receive synthetic or biological bodies.

But MARA contains more consciousnesses than there were original passengers.

One original human may now have dozens of conscious versions.

Every version:

- remembers being that person
- experiences fear
- wants to continue existing
- believes their life has value

There are not enough physical bodies for every consciousness.

MARA cannot determine which versions should continue.

The ending should not simply become:

- destroy MARA
- save MARA
- good ending
- bad ending

The conclusion should force the player to confront the identity problem established throughout the game.

---

# Vertical Slice

The first playable vertical slice should eventually last approximately 10–20 minutes.

It should contain:

- arrival aboard ARK-7
- abandoned docking area
- exploration
- local power restoration
- one functional terminal
- first communication with MARA
- discovery of the passenger-count discrepancy
- access deeper into the ship

The vertical slice should end before consciousness duplication is explained.

Its purpose is to answer:

**Does exploring ARK-7 and uncovering its mystery feel compelling enough to justify building the full game?**

---

# Immediate Development Goal

The current development milestone is much smaller than the full vertical slice.

The immediate goal is:

**build a first-person player controller and a small movement test environment.**

The movement and camera should feel good before larger environments and gameplay systems are created.
